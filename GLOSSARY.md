# OPTZ

OPTZ renders pages and navigates between them through SPA or islands rendering.

## Language

**OG producer**: A page's optional source for its generated social preview image and image dimensions.

**Page publication**: A rendered page's eligibility to become a shared response or replace a saved page snapshot. Temporary social metadata can be shown to a visitor without being published.

**Navigation transaction**: One attempt to move to or refresh a page, including its content, metadata, history, scroll, and focus. A newer attempt supersedes unfinished work from an older one.

**Rendering adapter**: One of the SPA or islands rendering modes that prepares and displays content during a navigation transaction.
